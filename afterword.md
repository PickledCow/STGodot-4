# Afterword
> WARNING: Wall of text
## A Kirby Shmup, huh?

Yep a Kirby shmup.

This is a kind of game that I've been wanting to make for quite a long time now, though the ideas brewing in my head were more based on events from the actual Kirby franchise.  
This idea of a Kirby shmup existed long before I even really truly started making any shmups so its like at least 6 year ago I had the desire to make something like this.  
However, my ideas were very clearly too grandiose in scale to be made and the core gameplay I didn't have any strong ideas for other than there being suck-spit and copy abilities so the idea was shelved for the time being.

## BHA 10 Approaches

A bit of time before BHA 10 was even announced, after playing some Vampire Survivors on a friend's computer, something about the absolute chaos managed to enchant me and I gained a desire to make something of that sort where I could let go of conventional sensibilities for gameplay and just go absolutely wild as I had felt I was being way too restrained with designs because I had this fear that it could not be fun so I just settled for a basic gameplay I knew worked.  

Development of the game was very slow with as I started thinking about it more and the idea just not really coming together.  I could not figure out how to balance the gameplay curve to not be unreasonably snowbally in either direction but still have your item choices and gameplay execution matter.  The ideation phase and prototyping continued a bit into the start of BHA 10, where the theme was announced to be "no theme".

Great, no theme huh.  I personally struggle a ton when given absolute freedom to do anything I want so I just continued to try to figure out a way to make this Vampire Survivors idea thing work for about half a month before giving up on the idea.  I needed a new idea for BHA 10 quick.  
So I dug through past ideas and after a bit of digging and brainstorming, there it was, the Kirby shmup concept from ages ago.  
It was perfect, the theme was "do anything" and what can Kirby do?

I've participated in most BHAs (Bullet Hell Artistry, a bi-yearly Touhou style shmup game jam) since BHA4, only missing out on BHA7 and BHA9, both of which had mechanical themes which had major alterations to the gameplay.  
BHA9 in particular was themed around having unique shot-types where I did get a bit into development before other commitments got in the way and I had to drop out.  
I finally had my chance to use some of the unique shottypes I had planned for BHA9.

## Core Design Goals
Going in, I knew that I wanted the suck + spit gameplay to be always viable in the same way that it is always possible to do so in a typical Kirby game.  As such, all the patterns were first designed and playtested with the base ability with the design, programming, and balance of the other copy abiliies done after the fact.

With the abilities, the abilities were chosen with consideration of unique theming and mechanics.  Each ability should have some unique way its played over another ability, even if it is strictly weaker or stronger than others in a balance sense.  This is one of the gripes I tend to have with ZUN's balancing where there are very often shottypes that are just completely pointless to use because another shot is basically just a better version of it.  
I feel that I've for the most part managed to achieve this design goal, though Sword I feel has the weakest design.  I didn't quite want to just reuse TD Youmu's shottype wholesale and wanted a near-pure melee shottype but I couldn't quite figure out how to give the player good survivability while still encouraging aggressive play without it being completely busted, and in the end I feel that Sword can be a pretty similar experience to Parasol with both being close range bullet clearing abilities.

## Development
### Music

Like always, I put some considerable amount of focus in music production.  This BHA marks the first time I really properly started to figure out how to use a DAW for once instead of just using Musescore as I did for all my past entries.  
Music production definitely took a considerable amount of development time with having to learn the workflow of FL Studios and figuring out how to use VSTs effectively. 

### Art
This is also the first time I tried to draw portraits for once, instead of just having funny MS Paint marisa.png or just stealing F2U sprites.  While I had some comfort already making spritework for stuff like items, or even with making player sprites, these were all done using shapes and not a single pen used. But really, isn't breaking stuff down into shapes like just how you draw anyways?  So I  manned up and went for it.  And?  I liked doing it.  
I've only barely tried to draw in the past but it just never stuck as I felt the amount of effort was just far too great but being half forced to do it now and I forgot that I actually did enjoy doing it.  Definitely am going to do this again in future entries.  Whether or not I'd draw outside of this not too sure yet.

### Gameplay
I had some doubts going in about how the gameplay could work but surprisingly it for the most part went quite smoothly with little of the issues I had invisioned such as just eating everything and there being nothing to dodge.  While I did kinda have issues like that, they were for the most part easy enough to solve by having attacks that were just simply not edible alongside the edible projectiles.

## The Crunch

Having spent a third of the time loitering around already, there was going to be some crunch and downscoping of the project compared to the vision in my head.  The original vision of the game had around 3 more bosses and a Yuyuko fight before the Mizuchi fight.

I originally had plans to have Fire and Hammer as abilities and bosses but I didn't quite have a good reason for the abilities to exist compared to other abilities. 

Fire would have had an afterburn mechanic but without stage sections and only bosses, this really didn't amount to any meaningful gameplay change that wasn't just a boring forwards shot.  
Hammer I also could not really come up with significant mechanical differences over Sword for the ability to exist and was cut early.  
The third ability would have been Ghost, a power that would let you turn invisible and have the boss aim at where you last cloaked.  With this power you would move behind the boss to "backstab" the enemy for massive amounts of damage, while also moving the player back down to the bottom of the screen and uncloaking them so they can repeat the process.  However, this would require significant accomodation of the patterns and was dropped fairly early, though there are some remenants of the plan with many patterns being still designed around this play being possible.

The Yuyuko fight cut came somewhat later into development and was primarily a result of running out of time but also contributing to the cut was  pacing concerns.  This also allowed me to save time from not having to make a song for fighting Yuyuko but in the end I think it was the right call for pacing as I feel having a third phase to that end gauntlet would be too much.

The main crunch though came with both the music and the art.

With the Mizuchi fight, I had originally wanted to write an original piece that was inspired by Magolor's first theme from Kirby as I was very attached to that "shit hit the ceiling" feeling the song had but I just could not get it out of my head while trying to make either a new song or some twisted remix of either of Yuyuko's themes.  So the decision was made to just have Magolor's theme.  At this point though I was just completely running out of time and as much as it shames me, I had to submit defeat and go grab someone's hard work at transcribing to figure out how the instruments were being used to greatly assist in the production of this song.  Major shout out to [kujiratr](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=08eJ5iXSwDQ) for the transcription as it is unlikely I would have gotten this out without their prior work.

Art wise, while I was having an absolute blast until making the boss rush sprites + Yuyuko (minus Marisa which was done by a friend as a guest artist), the pressure and burnout was really beginning to set in when I had to make my final two portraits with the possessed Yuyuko + Mizuchi and the Yuuma player portrait.  Though I did get something out the door, you can definitely see the lower effort put into them, especially the Mizuchi one, and I definitely am not as happy with how those turned out.

## Version 1.1?

No.  At least there's no real desire to.

I find it really hard to go back to a "finished" project and work on it more and would rather just move on to newer stuff.  Especially without some deadline to meet, I find it difficult to find any motivation to work on it as something like this is finished only when you call it finished and there's just always something more you can add or change.

But even in an hypothetical world where I had more development time I don't see myself adding too much more content from what I have and would probably just have had some more polish and attention to detail put in.  Main stuff that would have been different are:
- Redo the Mizuchi and Yuuma portraits, I really rushed those one out with pretty low quality.

- Perhaps more sound effects and/or make more myself instead of reusing ZUN ones.

- Different player character outfits like how Kirby gets hats would be cool to have.

- Generally more polish and attention to detail.

### Whoa 1 day extension?

Well I guess then it means v1.0.1 is happening.  Very little was done though because it was a 1 day extension so I focused on the easier stuff to do such as some more visual feedback on attacks and the BGM popup I completely forgot to add in the initial release.  
For a moment during making the patch, I almost wanted to add a Ghost ability but I deemed that to be a bit too beyond the scope of what I wanted to do for a quick 1 day extension patch because at this point I was pretty exhausted.
